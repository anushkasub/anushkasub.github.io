I am a second-year Master’s student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at The University of Texas at Austin, where I am fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Mattan Erez](https://lph.ece.utexas.edu/merez/MattanErez/MattanErez). I have also had the opportunity to collaborate closely with [Prof. Lizy John](https://users.ece.utexas.edu/~ljohn/). My research interests are in heterogeneous hardware and memory systems for emerging workloads.

Before graduate school, I spent two and a half years at Arm, working on CPU architecture extensions at the OS-hardware interface. I received my B.E. in Electronics & Communication Engineering from R.V. College of Engineering, Bengaluru.
